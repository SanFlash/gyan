Impact & Reporting

Figures with context. Progress with accountability.

GSSKS's programme records include reach and capacity figures across different reporting periods. Each figure below is presented with its relevant year and context.

ORGANISATIONAL REACH~1,200

Children reportedly integrated

GSSKS reports that approximately 1,200 children have been integrated into the social mainstream over several years. The reporting period and calculation method are not specified.

2023–24 ANNUAL REPORT100

Stated capacity across four units

25 children per unit × four units. This is a stated capacity for that reporting context, not current occupancy.

2022–23 ANNUAL REPORT269

Stated combined capacity for two units

The 2022–23 programme record lists 199 places for Unit 1 and 71 for Unit 2. This historical figure differs from the later four-unit figure.

How to read these figuresThese are not comparable measures: one is an organisation-reported cumulative reach estimate, while the other two are annual-report capacity figures. None should be described as the number of children currently residing at the centres.
Programme Activities

A closer look at the work carried out across our centres.

Daily care

Meals, clothing, footwear, toiletries, safe accommodation and age-appropriate arrangements.

Health & nutrition

Routine medical check-ups, immunisation, first-aid kits and balanced meals.

Education

School support, bridge learning, textbooks, stationery, computer classes and annual examinations.

Life skills

Counselling, value education, behaviour support, vocational exposure and employment-oriented training.

Recreation

Sports, cultural events, yoga, plantation, educational visits, picnics and festival celebrations.

Protection systems

Documented CWC/JJB interactions, inspections, legal-literacy activity and rehabilitation follow-up.
