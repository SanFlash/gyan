About GSSKS · Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh

Where Every Child Finds Home.

“Together We Shelter, Together We Care.” Gyan Path Shiksha Evam Samaj Kalyan Samiti is a registered non-governmental organisation based in Bhopal. GSSKS has a long-standing commitment to the care, protection, rehabilitation and holistic development of children, with particular attention to communities affected by the Bhopal Gas Tragedy.

Our Background

Working with communities affected by the Bhopal Gas Tragedy.

Families in Bhopal's gas-affected communities continue to face long-term health, social and economic challenges. Women and children can be especially vulnerable to chronic health conditions, economic hardship, limited access to education, emotional trauma and neglect.

GSSKS works to create a safe and nurturing environment in which children can be protected, supported and given opportunities to heal and grow. The organisation places child protection and rehabilitation within the wider context of community resilience and social protection.

Our Aim

Care, protection and opportunity.

To provide care, protection and opportunities for children in need; strengthen juvenile justice systems; and bring healing, support and hope to families affected by the gas tragedy—empowering them towards a better and more secure future.

GSSKS's approach is aligned with the policies and framework of the Department of Women and Child Development and guided by the Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act.

Our Work in the Community

Support that reaches beyond the shelter.

GSSKS works across vulnerable communities and alongside child-protection systems.

Open Shelter Home

Care, shelter and rehabilitation support for children in need of care and protection.

Counselling & Emotional Support

Support for children affected by trauma, family distress and difficult circumstances.

Community Outreach

Outreach activity in gas-affected settlements to identify children who may be at risk and need support.

Child-protection Coordination

Support and coordination with Child Welfare Committees (CWC) and Juvenile Justice Boards (JJB), as part of its child-protection approach.

Awareness

Awareness campaigns on child rights, protection and health.

Education & Rehabilitation

Learning, guidance and rehabilitation-oriented support as part of the organisation's child-centred approach.

Core Programme

Open Shelter Homes for Boys

GSSKS operates four listed Open Shelter Home units in Bhopal's Chhola Road and Pushpa Nagar areas. Its programme activities include daily care, education, health, inspections and community engagement.
