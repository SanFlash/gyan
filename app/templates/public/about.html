About GSSKS

Gyan Path Shiksha Evam Samaj Kalyan Samiti.

Established in 1992, GSSKS is a Bhopal-based non-governmental organisation working for the welfare, protection and holistic development of children, women and young people, with a documented focus on the gas-affected area of Bhopal.

Background

Working alongside gas-affected communities

The client brochure describes GSSKS's work in Bhopal's gas-affected communities, where families continue to face health, social and economic challenges. It identifies children and women as groups requiring particular attention, including support related to child protection, education, rehabilitation and social protection.

The organisation describes its work as being aligned with the Department of Women and Child Development and the Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act.

Aim

Protection, care and opportunity

To provide protection, care and opportunities for children in need, strengthen juvenile justice systems, and bring healing, support and hope to families affected by the Bhopal Gas Tragedy—empowering them towards a better and more secure future.
