About Gyanpath

Community development with a documented craft & livelihood footprint.

Gyanpath Shiksha Evam Samaj Kalyan Samiti is based in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh. Public records and organizational profiles describe work spanning education, social and economic upliftment, skill development and traditional craft.

Public profile

Documented organizational profile

A public LinkedIn profile describes Gyanpath as a Bhopal-based non-government organization with presence in adjoining districts including Vidisha, Raisen and Sehore, and says it has worked for education and social/economic upliftment. These statements are attributed to the public profile.

Government record

Stitching & embroidery cluster

The MSME SFURTI dashboard lists Gyanpath as implementing agency for a Madhya Pradesh Stitching Embroidery Cluster and records 410 artisans, Rs. 233.92 lakh GOI grant/NA share and Rs. 20.16 lakh IA/SPV share.
