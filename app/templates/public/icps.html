Integrated Child Protection Services

GSSKS's supplied organisational profile describes Open Shelter Home services for children in need of care and protection in Bhopal.

Explore Open Shelter Home programme →
Programme Framework

Care and protection under the Juvenile Justice framework.

The client brochure states that GSSKS operates Open Shelter Home units under the provisions of the Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015 and Central Rules, 2016, in the context of the Integrated Child Protection Scheme (ICPS), now under Mission Vatsalya. It identifies the Department of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh, as the supervising department.

Children Supported

Children in need of care and protection.

The brochure describes support for children aged 6–17, including children involved in begging or rag-picking, working children, children found wandering in vulnerable settlements or railway platforms, and children whose parents work as daily-wage labourers with unpredictable working hours.

Organisation-reported cumulative figureThe brochure states that approximately 1,200 children have been integrated into the social mainstream over several years. The document does not specify the reporting period or calculation method; this figure is therefore presented as an organisation-reported estimate, not an independently audited impact measure.
