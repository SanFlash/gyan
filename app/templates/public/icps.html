Our Core Work · Child Protection

Integrated Child Protection Services

The supplied GSSKS profile describes Open Shelter Home services for children in need of care and protection in Bhopal. The programme is presented in the context of ICPS and Mission Vatsalya and the Juvenile Justice framework.

Explore Open Shelter Home programme →
Programme Framework

Community-based care, protection and rehabilitation.

The organisation's brochure states that it operates Open Shelter Home units under the Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015 and Central Rules, 2016, within the Integrated Child Protection Scheme (ICPS), now under Mission Vatsalya. The Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Madhya Pradesh, is identified as the supervising department.

The PDF describes Open Shelters as non-institutional, community-based care settings intended to provide food, nutrition, education and vocational training to children in need.

Children the Programme Seeks to Support

Children in need of care and protection.

The organisation profile identifies children aged 6–17, including children involved in begging or rag-picking, working children, children found wandering in vulnerable settlements or on railway platforms, and children whose parents work as daily-wage labourers with unpredictable working hours.

Organisation-reported cumulative reachThe brochure states that approximately 1,200 children have been integrated into the social mainstream over several years. The document does not specify the reporting period, unique-child methodology or independent verification. This is therefore shown as an organisation-reported estimate, not an audited impact total.
Programme Components

A holistic support pathway for children.

The source document groups services around shelter and basic needs, education, health, counselling, juvenile-justice support, vocational training, recreation, family reintegration, outreach and monitoring.
