Programme Overview

Four Open Shelter Home units in Bhopal.

The organisation brochure describes four units operating in the Gas-Affected Area (Chhola) and Pushpa Nagar near Bhopal Railway Station. It describes day-long care and support for children in need of care and protection, including children aged 6–17.

Annual Activity Record

Activities documented from 2022–23 to 2024–25.

The following highlights are transcribed from the annual-report pages supplied by GSSKS. They describe activities recorded in those reporting years and should not be read as a guarantee that every activity is currently delivered at every unit.

ANNUAL REPORT · 2022–23

Health, learning and daily care

  • Routine medical check-ups and first-aid kits for children.
  • Three meals daily, according to the diet plan.
  • Vaccination reported for students aged 12–14 years.
  • Indoor and outdoor games, with prizes for participation in games and cultural activities.
  • Uniforms and stationery distributed to children.
  • Digital learning and computer classes provided.
  • Inspections and interactions with officials and child-welfare representatives are documented in the report.
ANNUAL REPORT · 2023–24

Learning, exposure and community participation

  • Daily activities began with morning prayer; three healthy meals were reported.
  • Knowledge visit to Rani Kamalapati Palace on 19 July 2023.
  • Environmental activities and Independence Day celebrations.
  • Child Welfare Committee representatives visited the shelter; uniforms, books and learning materials were distributed.
  • Medical check-ups, first aid, indoor/outdoor recreation and computer learning continued.
  • The report states four units with a capacity of 25 children each (100 total) for that reporting year.
ANNUAL REPORT · 2024–25

Health, skills and wellbeing

  • Three healthy meals daily and routine medical check-ups.
  • Employment-oriented training for children.
  • Painting and rangoli activities and International Yoga Day celebration.
  • Diwali celebration and visits to a nearby park.
  • Cleanliness drives among shelter-home students.
  • Visit by SJPU In-charge Inspectors Kiran and Aarti.
  • Visit and discussion with Dr. Rahul Sharma, clinical psychologist, J.P. Hospital.
  • Computer classes, annual examinations, plantation, cultural activities and counselling sessions are shown in the report.
Reported Programme Reach

Figures from the supplied reports

The 2023–24 annual report states a capacity of 25 children per unit across four units (100 total). The organisation brochure separately states that approximately 1,200 children have been integrated into the social mainstream over several years. The brochure does not specify the period or calculation method for the latter figure.
