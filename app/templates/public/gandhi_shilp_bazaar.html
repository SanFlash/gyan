The initiative

A public platform for traditional craft.

Gyan Path Shiksha Evam Samaj Kalyan Samiti conducted Gandhi Shilp Bazaar from 11 to 17 September 2024 at Urban Haat, Gauhar Mahal, Bhopal, under the MSS of the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India.

The seven-day exhibition brought together 50 artisans and welcomed members of the public. It created a shared space for artisans to present their work, engage with visitors and generate sales.

Reported sales during the exhibition exceeded ₹14,95,577.

Crafts and products

A diverse range of handmade work.

The exhibition showcased jute products, Assam bamboo products, dry-stone work, handmade bedsheets, Madhubani paintings, silk sarees, jute and POP pottery, patchwork, zari work, paintings, and woollen or fibre-yarn crafts.

The range brought together textile, decorative, pottery, painting and natural-material craft traditions in one public exhibition setting.

Programme outcomes

Visibility, engagement and sales.

The exhibition connected participating artisans with public visitors and gave their products a dedicated place for display and sale. Its reported sales figure provides a measurable view of the commercial activity generated during the seven-day event.
