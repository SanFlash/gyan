Gandhi Shilp Bazaar

The supplied GSSKS website brief documents a seven-day Gandhi Shilp Bazaar programme conducted by the organisation under the MSS of the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, at Urban Haat, Gauhar Mahal, Bhopal.

The brief reports participation by 50 artisans and sales of more than ₹14,95,577.

Products documented

Crafts represented

Products mentioned in the supplied documentation include jute products, Assam bamboo products, dry stone, handmade bedsheets, Madhubani paintings, silk sarees, jute and POP pottery, patchwork, zari work, paintings, and woollen/fibre yarn crafts.
