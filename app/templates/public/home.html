Documented Programme History

Activities recorded across three annual reports.

The supplied PDF records health and learning activities in 2022–23; educational visits, Environment Day, Independence Day and legal-literacy activity in 2023–24; and employment-oriented training, Yoga Day, Diwali and wellbeing activities in 2024–25.

Daily care & learning
